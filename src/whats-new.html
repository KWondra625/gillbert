What's New

The story of Gillbert, from a single chat window to a full fishing companion.

🚀 Current Version
Version 5
Nov 2026
Hard Water
A home page that fits on one screen, a phone alert for every catch, and a Gillbert that dresses for the season.
🏠 A New Home Page
  • Everything you use most on one screen: log a catch, browse catches, Fish of Fame and Ask Gillbert
  • The rest is tucked into the tackle box, or the ice shack when the lakes freeze over
  • "On the line" scrolls the latest five catches along the bottom, with angler photos; tap one to open it
  • "Remember when?" digs up a past catch from this time of year, a new one every day
  • On a laptop the page spreads out side by side
❄️ Hard Water & Open Water
  • November to March, Gillbert goes hard water: an orange tip-up flag springs up on the home page, the menu becomes the ice shack, and every page turns a cold steel blue
  • April to October it's open water: a bobber, the tackle box and the classic blue
🔔 Catch Notifications
  • Get a phone alert every time someone logs a new catch, through the free ntfy app
  • A setup page walks you through it in four steps, with a one-tap subscribe button
📸 Uploads That Keep Up
  • Every file you upload shows its own result; the good ones are kept if one fails, and you can retry just the failed ones
  • Photos and videos convert in the background, with a ⏳ tile on the catch until they're ready
🔍 Small Polish
  • A link to a catch that's gone now says "that one got away" and points you back to the catch log
  • Admin Settings: the home page's welcome line, Remember when picks and tease lines, and a season preview
📖 History
Version 4
Sept 2026
Meet the Crew
Anglers get profiles, photos, and crews of their own — plus admin tools for every corner of the record book.
🎣 Angler Profiles
  • Every angler can now have a profile photo, cropped to a perfect circle right in the browser
  • Photos show up on Fish of Fame's Top Anglers, catch details, and the anglers list
  • Anglers can update their own photo from a new "My Profile" page, no admin access required
  • Tag anglers into casual crews or families (e.g. "the original crew") and filter Fish of Fame and the catch log by group
🛠️ Full Admin Control
  • Anglers can now be added and edited directly by an admin, not just created through chat
  • Fish species roster expanded and fully admin-managed — add, edit, mark active/inactive, link to official DNR pages
  • Bodies of water are admin-managed too, and can pull real details (name, location, hydro type) straight from the Wisconsin DNR by name or WBIC
  • Catch Chat got smarter at matching typo'd or partial species names to the right record instead of creating duplicates
💬 A Smarter Gillbert
  • Catch Chat and Ask Gillbert now know who they're talking to and greet you by name
  • Gillbert remembers your most recent catch and can bring it up naturally in conversation
📸 Media
  • Swipeable, full-screen photo/video gallery on catch details — browse a catch's media like a mini slideshow
🔍 Small Polish
  • Admin-only Pending Review filter to instantly see what's still awaiting verification
  • Simpler, faster time entry when logging a catch manually
  • A new Features & Functionality page — see everything Gillbert can do, all in one place
Version 3
July 2026
The Official Record Book
A complete overhaul of the backend, AI, and everything in between.
🏗️ Under the Hood
  • Migrated from Airtable to a self-hosted PostgreSQL database. Faster, cheaper, more flexible, and ours to own
  • Switched the AI powering Gillbert from OpenAI to Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Anthropic)
  • All API workflows rebuilt and running on a self-hosted n8n instance on a Raspberry Pi
  • Photos and videos stored in Azure Blob Storage with secure, time-limited access links
  • App secured behind Cloudflare Zero Trust, only invited anglers can get in
💬 Chat with Gillbert
  • Replaced the raw n8n chat interface with a custom-built and styled Catch Chat window
  • Ask Gillbert introduced. A separate AI chat to ask any question about the full catch history
  • Gillbert knows the entire record book and can give thoughtful, personalized answers
  • Catch numbers in chat responses are clickable links straight to the catch details
🏆 Fish of Fame
  • Leaderboard showing the biggest catches of all time, ranked by length
  • Per-species records and the record holder for every fish species in the books
  • Top Anglers ranking by total catch count and biggest fish
✏️ Catch Management
  • Edit any catch after it's been logged. Fix a length, update the date, edit the notes, etc.
  • Manual catch logging form as an alternative to chatting with Gillbert
  • Admin catch verification. Mark catches as officially reviewed and verified
👤 Who Did What
  • Catches and uploaded media now remember who logged and who uploaded them
  • See who created and last updated a catch, right on its details page
  • Anglers can edit their own catches (or ones they logged) without needing the admin PIN, until it's verified
📸 Media Improvements
  • Apple HEIC photos and MOV videos are automatically converted on upload for universal viewing
  • Admin "Manage Media" mode to delete individual photos or videos from a catch
  • Photo and video counts update instantly after each deletion
🔍 Browse & Discover
  • Filter catches by angler, fish species, and body of water. Mix and match
  • Active filters and search terms are remembered when navigating between pages
  • Friendly "no results" message when a search comes up empty, no more blank cards
🔒 Admin Mode
  • Admin mode hidden in plain sight
  • All destructive actions (deleting, editing, verifying) are gated behind admin mode
