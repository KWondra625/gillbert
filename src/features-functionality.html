About Gillbert

Features & Functionality

Everything Gillbert can do today, all in one place.

🏠 Home & Seasons
  • Everything you use most fits on one screen: log a catch, browse catches, Fish of Fame and Ask Gillbert
  • The rest lives in the tackle box (April to October) or the ice shack (November to March): notifications, your profile, what's new and this page
  • "On the line" runs along the bottom: the latest five catches with angler photos; tap one to open it
  • "Remember when?" brings back a past catch from this time of year, a new one every day
  • Hard water, November to March: a tip-up flag, the ice shack and cold steel blues on every page. Open water the rest of the year: a bobber, the tackle box and the classic blue
  • Get a phone alert every time someone logs a new catch, with the free ntfy app (set it up from the tackle box or the ice shack)
  • The same bar tops every page: tap "Gillbert" to go home, and the tackle box or ice shack is always one tap away
💬 Chat with Gillbert
  • Log a catch in plain conversation — Gillbert asks the right questions and captures the details
  • Ask Gillbert: a separate AI chat for questions about the full catch history, with personalized, thoughtful answers
  • Gillbert knows who it's talking to and greets you by name
  • Remembers your most recent catch and can bring it up naturally in conversation
  • Catch numbers in any chat response are clickable links straight to that catch's details
  • ✨In ice season, Catch Chat asks which tip-up it came on and what bait: "got him on KW 24-02 with a sucker" fills both in
  • ✨Ask Gillbert answers gear questions: "best bait on Kegonsa?", "what's on KW 24-02?"
🐟 Catch Management
  • Log a catch by chatting with Gillbert or filling out a quick manual form
  • Edit any catch after it's logged — fix a length, update the date, tweak the notes (the Edit button sits next to the catch's title)
  • Each catch's page leads with the fish, who caught it, where and when, next to a big angler photo
  • ✨Log or edit a catch with the tip-up it came on and the bait; the catch shows that tip-up's setup (and photo) as it was that day
  • Anglers can edit their own catches (or ones they logged) without the admin PIN, until it's verified
  • Admin verification marks a catch as officially reviewed (under More at the bottom of the catch)
  • Admin filter to instantly see which catches still need review
🚩 Gear
  • ✨A Gear page in the ice shack (the tackle box in summer) lists the crew's tip-ups, each with its owner, and its model and color if you like
  • ✨Each tip-up's setup: line, leader, hook, beads and flash, picked as you type from what the crew already uses
  • ✨A photo of the setup: tap it for the full-size view and "Download original"; a 📷 on the Gear page marks tip-ups that have one
  • ✨Every change is kept in the tip-up's history: who changed what, and when
  • ✨Retire a lost or broken tip-up: its name, history and fish stay. One that never caught anything can be deleted
  • ✨Gear Stats: fish by tip-up, bait, hook, line, leader, beads and flash, with the biggest fish on each, for one species or lake, this season or all time
📸 Media
  • Attach photos and videos to any catch, with "＋ Add photos" right at the top of its photos
  • Apple HEIC photos and MOV videos are automatically converted for playback on any device
  • Uploads show a result for every file, keep the good ones if one fails, and let you retry just the failed ones
  • Conversion happens in the background: a ⏳ tile shows on the catch until the photo or video is ready, and a ⚠️ tile with Retry if it couldn't be converted
  • Swipeable photo/video gallery — swipe or arrow through a catch's media like a mini slideshow
  • Admin "Manage media" mode (under More) to remove individual photos or videos
  • Catch pages load small thumbnails instead of full-size photos, and videos show a poster frame, so busy catches open fast
  • Download original from the full-screen view saves the photo as it was uploaded
  • Admin Media page: every upload with its status, thumbnails to generate for older files, older Apple-format files to convert, and a storage check for stray files
🔍 Browse & Discover
  • The catch log is grouped by day: Today, Yesterday, This week, then by month
  • Search and filter the full catch log by angler, fish species, and body of water — mix and match
  • Filter by angler group too — see just one family's or crew's catches
  • Active filters and searches are remembered as you navigate around
  • Fish of Fame: biggest catches of all time, per-species records, and a Top Anglers leaderboard
  • Fish of Fame can be filtered by angler group too — every leaderboard narrows to just that crew
👤 Anglers
  • Anglers can now be added and edited directly by an admin, not just created through chat
  • Every angler can have a profile photo, cropped to a perfect circle right in the browser
  • Photos show up on Fish of Fame's Top Anglers, catch details, and the anglers list
  • Anglers can update their own photo from a new "My Profile" page, no admin access required
  • Tag anglers into casual crews or families (e.g. "the original crew") for the group filters above
🗺️ Reference Data
  • A managed roster of fish species — add new ones, mark them active or inactive, track aliases and family info
  • Each species can link out to its official Wisconsin DNR page
  • A managed roster of bodies of water, with the same active/inactive and admin controls
  • Adding a new body of water can pull real details straight from the Wisconsin DNR's public waterbody data by name or WBIC, instead of typing everything by hand
  • Catch Chat matches typo'd or partial species names to the right record instead of creating duplicates
🔒 Admin Mode
  • Admin mode is hidden in plain sight — no visible "admin login" button for everyone to see
  • A Settings page in the admin tray: the home page's welcome line, Remember when picks and tease lines, and a preview of either season (saved on that device for now)
  • ✨Gear Tags: rename, hide, delete, or merge look-alike tags ("30lb dacron" into "Dacron 30 lb"), moving everything that uses them
  • Every admin-only action (verifying, deleting, managing reference data) is gated behind it — anglers can still edit their own unverified catches without it, as noted above
🏗️ Under the Hood
  • Self-hosted PostgreSQL database — fast, flexible, and fully owned
  • Powered by Claude (Anthropic) for all of Gillbert's conversational AI
  • All backend workflows run on our own self-hosted n8n server
  • Photos and videos live in Azure Blob Storage behind secure, time-limited links
  • The entire app sits behind Cloudflare Zero Trust — only invited anglers get in
