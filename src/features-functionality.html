🧰 Features & Functionality

Everything Gillbert can do today, all in one place.

💬 Chat with Gillbert
  • Log a catch in plain conversation — Gillbert asks the right questions and captures the details
  • Ask Gillbert: a separate AI chat for questions about the full catch history, with personalized, thoughtful answers
  • ✨Gillbert knows who it's talking to and greets you by name
  • ✨Remembers your most recent catch and can bring it up naturally in conversation
  • Catch numbers in any chat response are clickable links straight to that catch's details
🐟 Catch Management
  • Log a catch by chatting with Gillbert or filling out a quick manual form
  • Edit any catch after it's logged — fix a length, update the date, tweak the notes
  • Anglers can edit their own catches (or ones they logged) without the admin PIN, until it's verified
  • Admin verification marks a catch as officially reviewed
  • ✨Admin filter to instantly see which catches still need review
📸 Media
  • Attach photos and videos to any catch
  • Apple HEIC photos and MOV videos are automatically converted for playback on any device
  • ✨Swipeable photo/video gallery — swipe or arrow through a catch's media like a mini slideshow
  • Admin "Manage Media" mode to remove individual photos or videos
🔍 Browse & Discover
  • Search and filter the full catch log by angler, fish species, and body of water — mix and match
  • ✨Filter by angler group too — see just one family's or crew's catches
  • Active filters and searches are remembered as you navigate around
  • Fish of Fame: biggest catches of all time, per-species records, and a Top Anglers leaderboard
  • ✨Fish of Fame can be filtered by angler group too — every leaderboard narrows to just that crew
👤 Anglers
  • ✨Anglers can now be added and edited directly by an admin, not just created through chat
  • ✨Every angler can have a profile photo, cropped to a perfect circle right in the browser
  • ✨Photos show up on Fish of Fame's Top Anglers, catch details, and the anglers list
  • ✨Anglers can update their own photo from a new "My Profile" page, no admin access required
  • ✨Tag anglers into casual crews or families (e.g. "the original crew") for the group filters above
🗺️ Reference Data
  • ✨A managed roster of fish species — add new ones, mark them active or inactive, track aliases and family info
  • ✨Each species can link out to its official Wisconsin DNR page
  • ✨A managed roster of bodies of water, with the same active/inactive and admin controls
  • ✨Adding a new body of water can pull real details straight from the Wisconsin DNR's public waterbody data by name or WBIC, instead of typing everything by hand
  • ✨Catch Chat matches typo'd or partial species/lake names to the right record instead of creating duplicates
🔒 Admin Mode
  • Admin mode is hidden in plain sight — no visible "admin login" button for everyone to see
  • Every destructive or administrative action (editing, verifying, deleting, managing reference data) is gated behind it
🏗️ Under the Hood
  • Self-hosted PostgreSQL database — fast, flexible, and fully owned
  • Powered by Claude (Anthropic) for all of Gillbert's conversational AI
  • All backend workflows run on a self-hosted n8n instance on a Raspberry Pi
  • Photos and videos live in Azure Blob Storage behind secure, time-limited links
  • The entire app sits behind Cloudflare Zero Trust — only invited anglers get in
