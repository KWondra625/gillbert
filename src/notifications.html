🔔 Catch Notifications

Get a buzz on your phone every time someone logs a new catch.

1Get the ntfy app

Gillbert sends notifications through ntfy, a free app with no account and no sign-up.

2Subscribe to Gillbert's catches

On your phone, tap the button. It opens ntfy and subscribes you.

Subscribe in the ntfy app
Button didn't work? Add it by hand
  1. In ntfy, tap + to add a subscription.
  2. Topic name: gillbert-catches
  3. Turn on Use another server and enter: https://ntfy.builtbykw.net
  4. Tap Subscribe.
3Allow notifications

When your phone asks whether ntfy may send notifications, tap Allow.

On Android, ntfy keeps a small "listening" notification so new catches arrive right away. That's normal.

4That's it

Each new catch shows who caught what. Tap the notification to open the catch in Gillbert for the rest.

Edits to existing catches don't send notifications. To stop them, delete the subscription in ntfy.
